Hi, I'm Viola. I sing, I dance, and I run a little AI consulting thing in New York.

I'm a sucker for patterns. I love dealing with numbers, and there's a decent chance you'll catch me playing Sudoku on the subway, fully zoned out between stops. I'm interested in the relationship between structure and feeling: data and logic, visual language, performance, choreography, and the design of social experiences.

Mostly I'm just curious. About people, about cities, about whatever's at the edges of things. If you're curious too, we'll probably get along.
